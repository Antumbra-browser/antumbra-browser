Strict tracking protection, ad blocking, cookie banner rejection. Works with nearly every site.

Strict
Everything in Standard, plus fingerprinting protection and aggressive parameter stripping. Occasionally breaks logins on complex sites.
Better privacy, some site breakage possible.
Blackout
Maximum hardening. Resist Fingerprinting on, WebRTC restricted, pure-black chrome. Breaks more sites intentionally.
Highest privacy, highest breakage. Use per-site relaxation when needed.

Password vault

Antumbra includes a built-in password manager.

Set a primary password to encrypt your saved passwords with a key only you know.

Without one, your saved passwords are encrypted with a key stored next to them. Anyone with access to your profile can read them.

You can set a primary password later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Passwords.

How updates work

Antumbra does not yet have a built-in updater. Updates are delivered through your package manager or as direct downloads from GitHub Releases.

Two background connections you should know about:

Remote Settings (Mozilla): periodically downloads certificate revocation data, tracking protection lists, and cookie banner rejection rules. No data is uploaded. Required for security and for cookie banner blocking to work.

Safe Browsing (Google): local malware and phishing lists are checked against your visits. The per-download remote lookup that sends URLs to Google is disabled. Only local list checks run.

Both are disclosed in the privacy policy. Neither is telemetry.
