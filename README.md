# Antumbra

A privacy-first web browser. Firefox fork, desktop and Android.

> ## Nothing follows you here.

Privacy on by default, not by checkbox.

## The name

*Pronounced an-TUM-bra.*

In optics, the **antumbra** is the region beyond the umbra, where the blocking
body no longer covers the whole light source and instead appears as a dark disc
ringed by light. Stand in it and you see the entire world around the thing in
your way, while everything trying to come back at you through the center is
stopped.

That is what a content blocker does.

## Modes

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| Standard | Sensible privacy defaults that do not break the web |
| Strict | Aggressive blocking and fingerprinting resistance |
| **Blackout mode** | Maximum adblocking and tracking protection |
| **Totality window** | Built-in Tor window, powered by Tor |
