# Antumbra: brand system

**Status: decided.** Research date 2026-09-18, updated 2026-09-18 with final
decisions.

Not legal advice. Before any public launch or merchandise, pay a trademark
attorney for a real Class 9 and Class 42 clearance opinion. Everything below is
open-source research, not a clearance search.

---

## 1. The name

# Antumbra

**Pronounced an-TUM-bra.** Stress on the second syllable. Four syllables, and
expect people to mistype it as "Anumbra" at first, so register the common
misspellings if budget allows.

### The name story

In optics, an eclipse has three shadow regions. The **umbra** is total darkness.
The **penumbra** is partial shade. The **antumbra** is the region beyond the
umbra, where the blocking body no longer covers the whole light source and
instead appears as a dark disc surrounded by a complete ring of light. That is
an annular eclipse, the "ring of fire."

Stand in the antumbra and you see the entire world around the thing in your way.
The blocker sits dead center, solid and opaque, and everything trying to come
back at you through that center is stopped. Light gets out. Nothing gets in.

That is exactly what a content blocker does, and it is a story no competitor in
this category can tell. It also keeps the astronomical family from the original
Umbra concept, so none of the earlier design thinking is wasted.

Short form for casual use: **Antumbra**. Do not abbreviate to "Anti" or "AU".

## 2. Why not Umbra

Kept here as the rationale of record.

"Umbra" was not legally blocked, but it was commercially dead on arrival. At
least five other privacy browsers already use the name, three with live public
sites, and [umbra-browser.org](https://umbra-browser.org/) is **also a Firefox
fork with tiered privacy levels**. An [Umbra Browser entry already exists on
AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/umbra-browser), which is the
single best discovery surface in this category. Two [Umbra](https://umbravpn.io/)
[VPNs](https://umbravpn.app/) also ship under the name.

Every clean domain was gone (umbra.com, .org, .app, .net, .dev, getumbra.\*,
umbrabrowser.com/.app/.online), as were the GitHub handles `umbra`,
`umbrabrowser`, `Umbra-Project` and `UmbraProjects`.

On trademark: no live US registration for bare UMBRA covering browser software
was found, but the neighborhood was crowded. The closest hazard was
**UMBRA TECHNOLOGIES, Reg. 6119133** (Umbra Technologies HK), Class 38, covering
Internet access and data transmission services, from a company selling VPN and
firewall products. **UMBRA SECURITY, Ser. 99079793**, a pending application for
cybersecurity consultation, crowded Class 42 further. **UMBRA, Reg. 3893125**
(Umbra LLC, Class 20 home decor) owns umbra.com and is the kind of well-funded
consumer brand that sends letters. A cancelled **UD UMBRADATA** anti-spyware
mark showed the space had already been tried.

bias-lighting software is a genuine Class 9 data point, but it is a workable name
where Umbra was not.

## 4. Feature names

### 4.1 Blackout mode: KEEP

The maximum adblocking and tracking protection tier.

No software product meaningfully owns the phrase. USPTO BLACKOUT marks exist in
quantity but sit in unrelated goods: radiation shields for electronic products
([Survivor 21 LLC, Ser. 88028936](https://uspto.report/TM/88028936), Cl. 9),
BLACKOUT VR, BLACKOUT BARBELL, BLACKOUT CANNABIS CO., BLACKOUT DESIGNS,
BLACKOUT BEACON, THE BLACKOUT CLUB. A descriptive feature name inside a larger
product is a weak mark and rarely worth anyone's enforcement budget.

Minor semantic drag: "blackout" already means a broadcast restriction and a power
outage, and "blackout period" is a term of art at the USPTO itself. Not
disqualifying.

**Do not** attempt to register it as a standalone mark.

### 4.2 Totality window: the built-in Tor window

Labeled in UI and docs as **"Totality window, powered by Tor."**

**Why the name changed, twice:**

- **"Shroud window" was dropped** because [Shroud is a shipping privacy-first
  browser on the Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9mtnnzt1fm36)
  that blocks trackers, ads and cookies. Same word, same category. On top of
  that, "shroud" is one of the best-known handles in gaming and streaming, so the
  search term is unwinnable in exactly your demographic, and a shroud is what a
  body is wrapped in, which is the wrong register for the feature meant to feel
  safest.
- **"Relay window" was dropped** because it collides with **Firefox Relay**,
  Mozilla's own email-masking privacy product. For a Firefox fork, borrowing a
  feature name from upstream's privacy product line is the worst possible
  collision: users would assume the two are related, and Mozilla's trademark
  position on its own product names is strong.

**"Totality" is the right answer.** Totality is the moment in an eclipse when the
light source is fully covered. It sits in the same astronomical family as
Antumbra, so the name system is internally consistent: you browse in the
antumbra, and when you need more, you go to totality.

**Conflict check on Totality:**

- No privacy, security, VPN, anonymity or browser product found using the name.
- Existing software using "Totality" is in unrelated fields: a
  [real-estate sales optimization app on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.totalityre.app)
  and [Totality Platform Inc.](https://www.totalityplatform.com/privacypolicy.html),
  a healthcare application.
- No Class 9 TOTALITY registration surfaced covering browser or privacy
  software. Confirm in the attorney search.
- As a feature name inside a larger product, exposure is low regardless.

**Verdict: low risk. Ship it.**

**Attribution requirement:** always pair the name with "powered by Tor" in the
UI, the docs and the store listings. Read the Tor Project's trademark policy
before shipping. Accurate descriptive statements that your product uses Tor are
permitted; do not use the Tor onion logo as your own mark and do not imply the
Tor Project endorses or maintains this browser.

## 5. Taglines

**Primary, locked:**

> ## Nothing follows you here.

Plain language, no jargon, the entire value proposition in four words. Works on
the download page, an F-Droid blurb, a store listing and a sticker.

**Secondary:**

- **"Cast no shadow."** Ties to the eclipse concept and means leave no trace.
  Best set under the logo.
- **"Privacy on by default, not by checkbox."** The line that earns the technical
  audience, because it is their exact complaint about every other browser. Use it
  in the README, the comparison table and any launch post.

**Merch spare:** "Maximum is the default."

**Do not** reference Firefox by name in any tagline or in the product name.
Mozilla's trademark policy restricts using their marks in product names and
promotional taglines. "A Firefox fork" belongs in the technical description, not
the brand line.

## 6. Icon: the annular eclipse

**The mark is an annular eclipse: a solid dark disc centered inside a thin,
glowing amber ring.**

This is a change from the earlier total-eclipse concept, and it is the right one,
because an annular eclipse is literally what you see from inside the antumbra.
The icon and the name now say the same thing.

### Construction

- **Center:** a solid disc in `--void` `#0B0D10`, occupying roughly 62 to 66
  percent of the icon's diameter. This is the blocker. It is opaque and it is the
  focal point.
- **Ring:** a thin concentric ring in `--corona` `#FFB020`, stroke weight roughly
  8 to 10 percent of the icon diameter, with a soft outer glow falling off to
  transparent. Complete and unbroken: an annular eclipse rings all the way
  around.
- **Gap:** a small amount of background between disc and ring so the ring reads
  as separate, not as an outline on the disc.
- **Background:** transparent, or `--void` for contexts that need a filled tile.

### 16px readability requirement

**Non-negotiable: the mark must resolve at 16px.** Favicons, tab strips and
Android launcher badges give you nothing more.

- At 16px the mark must read as **one dark circle with one bright ring**. Nothing
  else. No text, no inner detail, no secondary shapes.
- The ring must stay at least 1 full pixel wide after rasterization at 16px. If
  the stroke weight above renders thinner, thicken the ring in the small-size
  variant rather than letting it break up into dashes.
- Ship hand-tuned variants at 16, 32 and 48px. Do not let the build downscale the
  512px asset and hope.
- Test on both a light and a dark tab strip. The amber ring carries the mark on
  dark; on light, the dark center carries it.

### Mode variants

The same disc carries the whole system.

| Mode | Mark |
|---|---|
| **Standard** | Dark disc, thin ring in `--ash` `#8B95A3`. Neutral. Nothing shouts. |
| **Strict** | Dark disc, full `--corona` `#FFB020` ring with glow. The default brand mark. |
| **Blackout mode** | **Pure black disc, hairline outline only, no ring and no glow.** The light is gone. Use `#000000` for the disc and a 1px `--penumbra` `#222933` hairline so the shape still has an edge on a black background. |
| **Totality window** | The ring closes to **full totality**: the amber ring narrows to a bright corona flare and three small `--relay` `#7C5CFF` dots sit evenly spaced on the ring, nodding to Tor's three-hop circuit. Violet immediately signals "this window is different." |

### Never use

Padlocks, shields, hooded figures, masks, Guy Fawkes, keyholes, fingerprints with
a slash through them, spy silhouettes, or anything in hacker-green on black.
Every one reads either as generic stock security art or as something a user's
antivirus should quarantine. **The single biggest branding risk for an
independent privacy browser is looking like malware**, and the icon is where that
judgment gets made in under a second.

## 7. Color palette

Unchanged from the approved version. Principle: trust comes from restraint and
contrast discipline. Badass comes from a near-black ground and exactly one
high-chroma accent. Two accents maximum.

### Dark theme (primary)

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--void` | `#0B0D10` | App background. Near-black with a blue cast, not pure `#000`. |
| `--umbra` | `#14181D` | Panels, toolbar, sidebar. |
| `--penumbra` | `#222933` | Borders, dividers, elevated surfaces. |
| `--ash` | `#8B95A3` | Secondary text, disabled states. |
| `--daylight` | `#E8ECF1` | Primary text. |
| `--corona` | `#FFB020` | **Primary accent.** The eclipse ring. Active tab, focus ring, primary buttons. |
| `--relay` | `#7C5CFF` | **Secondary accent.** Totality window chrome only. |
| `--signal` | `#35D07F` | Protected, verified, connected. |
| `--alarm` | `#FF4D4D` | Blocked count, certificate errors, insecure connection. |

The `--umbra` and `--penumbra` token names are kept deliberately. They are
accurate optical terms for the mid-tones between void and light, and they keep
the eclipse vocabulary running through the codebase.

Amber as the primary accent is the deliberate choice. Every privacy and security
brand is blue or purple: ProtonVPN, Tor, NordVPN, DuckDuckGo. Mullvad (yellow)
and Brave (orange) are the exceptions that prove warm works. Amber against
near-black is distinctive, reads as light in darkness, and carries a natural
high-alert undertone that suits a blocker.

Violet for the Totality window follows the convention users already know from
Firefox private windows and Tor Browser, so the window announces itself without a
tutorial.

### Light theme

| Token | Hex |
|---|---|
| `--void` | `#F7F8FA` |
| `--umbra` | `#FFFFFF` |
| `--penumbra` | `#DDE2E9` |
| `--ash` | `#5C6672` |
| `--daylight` | `#0B0D10` |
| `--corona` | `#B87400` |
| `--relay` | `#5B3FD9` |

`#FFB020` fails WCAG AA against white, so the light theme darkens the accent to
`#B87400`. **Do not reuse the dark-theme amber on light backgrounds.**

### Contrast

- `--corona` on `--void`: roughly 10:1. Safe for text and UI at any size.
- `--relay` on `--void`: roughly 5.4:1. Safe for large text and UI components.
  For body text on dark, lighten to `#9B85FF`.
- `--ash` on `--void`: roughly 7:1. Safe for secondary text.

### Mode color coding in chrome

- **Standard:** neutral, `--ash` accents.
- **Strict:** `--corona` amber accents.
- **Blackout mode:** chrome goes fully monochrome, pure `#000000` toolbar, no
  accent color at all. Removing the color is the strongest possible signal that
  everything else has been removed too.
- **Totality window:** `--relay` violet chrome throughout, matching the
  convention users already expect from private windows.

## 9. Before launch

Work top to bottom. Items 1 through 4 are cheap and should happen this week,
before anything else hardens around the name.

### Naming and identity

- [ ] **Register `antumbrabrowser.org`** as the canonical project domain. `.org`
      is the right TLD for a privacy browser: torproject.org, mozilla.org.
- [ ] **Register `antumbrabrowser.com`** and redirect it to the `.org`.
- [ ] **Register `getantumbra.com`** for download links and short URLs.
- [ ] **Create the GitHub org `antumbra-browser`.** Confirmed available. Also
      claim `antumbrabrowser` and `getantumbra` to prevent impersonation.
- [ ] Open a purchase inquiry on **`antumbra.com`**, which is openly listed for
      sale via Dynadot. Set a walk-away number first.
- [ ] Watch **`antumbra.org`** (registered 2026-08-23 via Tucows, currently
      empty). If it lapses, take it.
- [ ] Register defensive misspellings if budget allows: `anumbrabrowser.com`,
      `antumbrabrowser.net`.
- [ ] Claim the name on the surfaces that decide discovery: GitHub, F-Droid,
      AlternativeTo, Mastodon, and the relevant subreddits.

### Legal

- [ ] **Pay a trademark attorney for a full clearance search in Class 9
      (downloadable software) and Class 42 (SaaS and software development).**
      This is the single most important item on the list. Budget roughly 1,000 to
      2,000 USD. Do it before merchandise, before an app store listing, and
      before a donation page.
- [ ] Have the attorney specifically assess the three known prior uses of
      Antumbra: the **bias-lighting software startup** (closest, an actual Class 9
      software use), the **Eurorack module maker**, and **Philips Dynalite
      Antumbra** lighting panels.
- [ ] Have the attorney confirm **TOTALITY** and **BLACKOUT** are clear as
      feature names in context, even though neither will be registered.
- [ ] Read and comply with the **Mozilla trademark policy**. Do not use "Firefox"
      in the product name, the domain, the tagline, or the logo. "Based on
      Firefox" in the technical description is fine.
- [ ] Read and comply with the **Tor Project trademark policy**. Always say
      "powered by Tor" rather than implying endorsement, and do not adopt the
      onion logo as your own mark.
- [ ] Pick and document the license, and make sure the trademark position is
      stated separately from it. Code can be free while the name is not.

### Assets

- [ ] Produce the icon at 16, 32, 48, 128, 256 and 512px, **hand-tuned at 16, 32
      and 48**, not downscaled.
- [ ] Produce all four mode variants: Standard, Strict, Blackout, Totality.
- [ ] Test the 16px favicon against both a light and a dark tab strip.
- [ ] Define the palette as design tokens in one place so the light theme cannot
      accidentally ship the dark-theme amber.

### Repository

- [ ] **Rename the GitHub repository** from `Umbra-Browser` to
      `antumbra-browser` and move it under the new org. GitHub will redirect the
      old URL, but update every link in the README and docs anyway.
- [ ] Rename the `Shroud`/`Relay` window strings to `Totality` across the
      codebase **before the string freeze**, while it is still cheap.
- [ ] Update the local clone directory name to match.
